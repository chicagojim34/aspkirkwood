Hours, Drop-off & Map

Everything you need for a smooth arrival and pick-up. Only enrolled children are allowed at camp.

🕘Camp Hours

9:00 – 2:00

Regular camp hours, every day.

🚗Morning Drop-off

9:00 – 9:30

Every day. Staff is not on duty until 9:00 am.

👋First Day Pick-up

By last name of child:

  • A – F: 1:15 – 1:30
  • G – M: 1:30 – 1:45
  • N – Z: 1:45 – 2:00
ℹ️

Swim group pick-up times (after the first day). At pick-up on the first day you'll be told which swim group your child is in. That determines the pick-up time for the rest of the session:

1st swim group: 1:20 – 1:40  ·  2nd swim group: 1:45 – 2:00

Please read before you arrive

Traffic, parking & drop-off

🅿️Parking

No parking needed — just get in line facing Manchester. There is no parking behind Bopp Chapel.

🚦Traffic

Please do not enter Curran Ave. from Manchester Road. And please do not make left-hand turns onto Manchester Road — it causes a back-up for the pick-up line. Go around the block using Manchester Lane to the right.

🚘Drop-off & Pick-up

Parents, please stay in your car during drop-off and pick-up. Staff will be there to load and unload children.

🤒If your child feels unwell

If your child does not feel good for any reason, please have them stay home. You'll be notified immediately if your child has an accident or injury.
