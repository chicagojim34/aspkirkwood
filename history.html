Once upon a time… we had a dream

The story of Ackermann's Happy Times Swim Program — 1942 to now, in Kirkwood, Missouri.

Our founder

Alice F. Ackermann

Alice's love and passion was that all children should have the life skill of water safety and the freedom to develop as individuals, at their own pace, while building self-esteem and confidence.

Following in her mother's footsteps as a teacher, Alice Ackermann founded Happy Times Nursery School for children ages 2 to 5 on the second floor of her home in 1942 — only the second nursery school in St. Louis County. Kindergarten teachers and parents praised her work with children. She continued the school until 1975 when her husband retired, but activity continued with the summer swim camp well into her 90's.

Founded with the help of her husband, Al Ackermann.

In the beginning

How Ackermann's grew

1942

Happy Times Nursery School

Alice founds Happy Times Nursery School for children ages 2 to 5 on the second floor of her Kirkwood home — only the second nursery school in St. Louis County.

1949

The first blow-up pool

Hot St. Louis summers took Happy Times from pre-school to swim camp. One special area of concern for Alice was water safety, so in 1949 the Happy Times Swim Program came into being. It started off very small — just one inflatable pool.

Early days

Quacky's pond becomes Pool #3

Where our pet duck "Quacky" swam, we built our first in-ground pool — this pool is now our Pool #3. With the help of Al Ackermann Sr., Alice and Al started digging and building, with another pool soon to follow.

A side note: every day Quacky would lay an egg in the yard for our dog Pouchy. Unfortunately, one day someone left the gate open and Quacky disappeared.

Growing

The big pool, #4

As we grew, we built our second in-ground pool — our big Pool #4. Alice and Al worked hard to keep the swim camp running, first swimming with giant homemade filters and a heater. (The pipes now go through the walls instead of over the deck.)

1975

Nursery school closes, camp lives on

Alice continued the nursery school until 1975, when her husband retired — but the summer swim camp kept going strong, with Alice active well into her 90's.

2014

A brand-new beginners' pool

In 2014 we replaced our beginners' round Pool #1, building on the 2013 pools to give our youngest swimmers a safe, welcoming place to start.

Today

Ackermann's Swim Camp

Now doing business as "Ackermann's Swim Camp," the swim program continues just as "Mrs. A" — as we all referred to Mrs. Ackermann — wanted it to be. It is co-owned and run by Bev Shoop and son Michael Shoop, with the help of daughter Carol Shoop Carlstrom.

Three generations

A true family camp

Alice and Al's three children — Beverly Shoop, Janet Zucker and Al Ackermann Jr. — all taught and helped Alice run the camp until Janet and Al moved out of state to begin their careers. After a short time, Bev moved back to Kirkwood and continued to help Alice run the camp.

Bev, her husband DeVere, and their children Carol and Mike Shoop also grew up working at camp and continue to be a part of it today — Carol as assistant manager, and Mike as Co-Owner, Certified Pool Operator, teacher and pool maintenance, alongside their mom, Bev Ackermann Shoop.

A lasting legacy

Thousands of swimmers — and counting

Over the years the camp became known as Ackermann's Happy Times Swim Program, where thousands of children have learned water safety along with the fundamental swimming strokes. They've come from near and far — and children and grandchildren of past campers have also found a wonderful, rich learning experience here.

Many who took their first strokes at Ackermann's have gone on to compete at the high school, local team and collegiate levels. Several went on to set national records — including Alice's grandson Albert, who competed at the Olympics.
