Pools, House & Playground

Four heated pools and a big backyard full of fun — right in the heart of Kirkwood since 1942.

Our home

In the backyard of this house since 1942

Ackermann's Swim Camp has been in the backyard of this Kirkwood home since 1942, at 1044 Curran Ave, Kirkwood, MO 63122.

  • All pools are heated.
  • Certified Pool Operator and Red Cross certified teaching staff.
  • We meet all compliance requirements enforced by the St. Louis County Health Department.
  • Drop-off and pick-up right at the street — teachers help children in and out of your car.
