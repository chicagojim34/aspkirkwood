A note from our family

Welcome to our extended camp family

Dear Parent,

As we get ready to start our 84th year in business we want to thank you for being part of our extended camp family. In those years over 60,000 children from not only St. Louis, but also the United States as well as the world have learned how to swim in our incredibly unique camp. We owe so much of our longevity to those of you who come back year after year and spread the good word about Ackermann's.

Since 2023 we have handled our enrollment process in a much different way than in previous years — we've gone to an online enrollment set up only on our website, aspkirkwood.com. All the information and enrollment forms are located there. Please remember, if you want to enroll your children with friends you'll need to do it at the same time (or as close as possible) to ensure that the space is available.

We pride ourselves on having only hired from within during our entire time in business — all of our counselors were former campers themselves or close family friends of people who went to camp for years. Like many small businesses we sometimes face a staffing shortage, so we ask for your help. If you know someone who is 15 years old or older, please let them know what a rewarding and fun summer job working at Ackermann's could be. If interested, e-mail aspstaff@icloud.com.

Thanks for your support. See you this summer,

Bev Ackermann Shoop, Michael Shoop & Carol Shoop Carlstrom

Why families choose us

Teaching swimming & water safety since 1942

Dedicated to the development and growth of children in a caring, non-competitive setting.

🌊Water Safety First

Every camper learns the life skill of swimming and water safety. Our staff are Red Cross certified and led by a Certified Pool Operator.
