Camp & First Day Information

A friendly guide to help you and your camper get ready for a great first day and a smooth session.

On the first day

What to expect

👋Check-in

Children will be greeted at their cars by staff. Counselors check in your camper(s) on the first day. A yellow card will be attached to the basket they'll use the entire session to keep their belongings in. The staff will direct your children on where to go and what to do.

📝Important notes for counselors

If your child has any information the counselors need to be aware of — allergies, special needs, etc. — please send a note and any needed medication with specific details.

Pack the night before

What to bring every day

  • 2 towels and 2 swimsuits every day
  • A lunch and something to drink (water bottles can be refilled at our fountains)
  • An ice pack in the lunch box
  • A back-up suit and underwear for children prone to bathroom accidents
  • Something warm to wear over their suit on cool days
🏷️

Please mark towels, lunch and snacks — and any warm clothing — with your child's first name and last initial. This keeps their belongings together and prevents them from going home with other campers.

Mealtime

Lunch, snacks & allergies

🍎Lunch time

After the first swim, campers have an hour break before getting in the pool again. Once they've eaten, they can use the time to play on the playground.

🥪What to pack

Please pack an ice pack in lunch boxes. We recommend not packing yogurts or make-your-own pizzas — they make quite a mess and can go bad in the heat. Water fountains are available to refill bottles.

⚠️Food allergies

Children with food allergies can sit separately on the allergy blanket at lunch with a counselor. If their allergies are not severe they can sit with the other children. Please send a note.

👕What to wear to camp

Send your child in their swimsuit under their clothes. Please leave their shoes in the car. Send a back-up suit and underwear for children prone to bathroom accidents. If it's a cool day, send something warm to wear over their suit — remember, the pools are all heated, but it can be cold when they get out.

🧴Sunscreen

Please apply sunscreen before camp. We will supervise children after lunch while they reapply sunscreen.

🚙Going home with someone else

If your child is going home with somebody different than yourself, please send a note with your camper to give to the staff. Also make sure your child knows who they are going home with that afternoon to avoid any confusion.

🧦Lost & found

Send a note so the counselors can look for a lost item the next day. If the item has a first name and last initial, we'll put it in the child's basket to take home. Unnamed items go to lost and found, which is spread out every morning at the gate — a counselor will ask children if they've lost anything and let them look.

A family favorite

Visiting Day

We hold a visiting day on the last Friday of each 2-week session. Visiting day is not a normal camp day — it's for family and friends to come and watch what your children did during their time at camp.

Come any time between 9:00 am and 1:00 pm. Your child's teacher will show you what they learned, and then you'll all go home together. One-week sessions do not have a visiting day.

Health & safety

Keeping every camper safe

🤒Illnesses

Please be aware of any viruses or illnesses that may affect your child. We are especially concerned about water-borne pathogens in diarrhea. If your child does not feel well for any reason, please have them stay home. We abide by the St. Louis County Health Department's regulations concerning any outbreaks.

🚑In case of injury

In the case of a critical injury, you will be contacted and your child will be taken to the closest emergency room — or one recommended by rescuers as the situation warrants.

💳Refunds

If you cancel before May 1st, you will receive a full refund. If you cancel after May 1st, you forfeit your $200 deposit.

🚦

Traffic reminder: Please do not enter Curran Ave. from Manchester Road, and do not make left-hand turns onto Manchester Road from Curran Ave. Parents, please stay in your car during drop-off and pick-up time.
