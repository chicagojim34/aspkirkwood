Now hiring

Certified swimming instructors & Jr. Counselors

We are hiring swimming instructors and Jr. Counselors for Summer 2026. For our entire history we've hired from within — many of our counselors were campers themselves.

  • Did your children attend camp in the past and might be interested in working at Ackermann's? We'd love to hear from them.
  • We will pay for lifeguard certification for staff who work 7 weeks out of the summer.
  • We also have spots for Jr. Counselors ages 12 to 14 who can be helpers.
  • Applicants for instructor roles should be 15 years old or older.
